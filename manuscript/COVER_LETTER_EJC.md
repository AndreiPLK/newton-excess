Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, *Quantitative Newton inequalities: a sharp constant for the centred-square spectrum, and most of Sibuya's 1988 conjecture*, for publication in the Electronic Journal of Combinatorics.

The paper proves two quantitative strengthenings of Newton's inequality for the normalised coefficients of real-rooted polynomials. For the centred-square spectrum (the odd squares, each twice) the Newton excess is shown to exceed 4/5 for every case, with the constant sharp; the proof is complete. For the unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind, the inequality conjectured by Sibuya in 1988 (Ann. Inst. Statist. Math. 40, eq. 3.4) is proved for every index up to 1000, for the lower nine tenths of every row, and at the top of every row with at most 802 indices missing; the one region left open is stated exactly, together with the obstruction.

The proofs are computer-assisted: finite decompositions in which every error term is an explicit interval computed in certified arithmetic (FLINT/Arb), never an asymptotic estimate. In line with the journal's Artificial Intelligence Policy, I state that the proofs and the code were developed with the help of an AI assistant under my direction; every step has been checked by me, every certificate is a script with a logged run that a reader can repeat, each certificate was re-implemented by an independent validator, and two adversarial reviews per theorem are included in the public package (Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22282840; GitHub AndreiPLK/newton-excess). The manuscript contains enough detail for a human reader to check the argument, and all citations have been verified against their sources.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. A preprint of the same content is archived on Zenodo under the DOI above; it has not been submitted to arXiv.

I am an independent researcher and have no conflicts of interest to declare.
